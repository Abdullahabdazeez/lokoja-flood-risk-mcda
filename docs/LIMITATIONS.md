# Limitations

1. The product is a GIS/MCDA susceptibility assessment, not a hydraulic flood-depth, probability or return-period model.
2. Historical validation uses satellite-derived inundation/wetting evidence and is affected by sensor, timing, threshold and land-cover limitations.
3. The 2012 Landsat-derived event evidence is substantially smaller than the 2018 and 2022 Sentinel-1 evidence and should be interpreted cautiously.
4. DEM uncertainty is important in low-relief confluence terrain. The ±10 m stress test measures model sensitivity but is not a locally surveyed DEM-error estimate.
5. The original AHP pairwise matrix was unavailable; the documented weights and consistency ratio were retained, but the matrix was not recreated.
6. The original final-risk coefficients were not recovered. The R7B linear decomposition is descriptive only and must not be presented as the authoritative original risk equation.
7. Hazard and risk are distinct. A physically hazardous location can have lower population-informed risk where exposure/vulnerability is low.
