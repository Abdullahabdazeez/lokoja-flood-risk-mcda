# Limitations

- The model is a relative MCDA assessment rather than a hydraulic simulation of flood depth, velocity or duration.
- Results depend on criterion standardisation, thresholds and AHP weights.
- Population data are modelled estimates and may not capture seasonal movement or recent growth.
- OpenStreetMap infrastructure completeness varies.
- A 30-metre grid cannot represent all building- or parcel-level conditions.
- Flood hazard and risk classes should support screening and prioritisation, followed by field verification and engineering assessment.
