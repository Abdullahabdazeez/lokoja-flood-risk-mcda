# Final Interpretation Lock

The authoritative physical-hazard product is the R5C validation-supported fixed-threshold map.

The final FHI domain covers 3,353.08 km². High and Very High hazard jointly cover 1,136.71 km², while Very High alone covers 22.22 km². These areas are outcomes of fixed numerical FHI thresholds, not predetermined quantile shares.

The High + Very High classes capture 85.08% of the 2012 historical flood evidence, 86.64% of the 2018 evidence and 86.36% of the 2022 evidence.

The model is robust in ranking under AHP perturbation: the minimum Spearman correlation is 0.99666. Class agreement averages 91.21%, with a minimum of 76.01%. Under ±10 m DEM elevation offsets, class agreement remains about 85.4–85.5%.

Hazard must not be conflated with final risk. Within the R7B common domain, 1,125.64 km² is High or Very High physical hazard, but only 58.54 km² of that land remains High or Very High in the existing population-informed risk classification. Of High/Very High hazard land, 58.05% transitions to Low risk and 31.96% to Moderate risk. Existing risk is more strongly associated with population vulnerability (Spearman rho 0.62144) than with FHI (rho 0.35780).

The R7B fitted coefficients are descriptive diagnostics only. They are not the recovered original risk formula.
