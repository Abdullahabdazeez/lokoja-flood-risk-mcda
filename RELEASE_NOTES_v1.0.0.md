## First prepared release

This repository presents a GIS-based flood hazard, vulnerability and risk assessment for Lokoja LGA, Kogi State, Nigeria.

### Included

- Multi-criteria flood-hazard assessment
- Analytic Hierarchy Process weighting and consistency testing
- Population and building-density vulnerability analysis
- Final flood-hazard and flood-risk classification
- Population exposure by risk class
- Road, education and health-facility exposure assessment
- High and Very High hazard and risk hotspot maps
- Final categorical GeoTIFFs and selected GIS layers
- Technical report and result tables
- Results-review notebook
- Reproduction and repository-validation scripts
- Automated GitHub Actions validation

### Principal findings

- High and Very High flood hazard covered **1,305.10 km²**, or **38.97%** of the classified area.
- High and Very High final risk covered **110.39 km²**, or **3.30%** of the classified risk area.
- Approximately **71,195 people** were located in High or Very High final-risk zones.
- Approximately **779.89 km of roads** were exposed to High or Very High hazard.
- **7 educational facilities** and **21 health facilities** were exposed to High or Very High hazard.
- The AHP consistency ratio was **0.0093**, indicating consistent pairwise weighting.

### Interpretation

The project provides a strategic screening and prioritisation framework. The results should be combined with hydraulic modelling, field verification, infrastructure surveys and local planning evidence before engineering or regulatory implementation.
