# Release Notes — Validated Reconstruction

## Major analytical corrections

This release supersedes the earlier Lokoja flood-hazard classification.

### Corrected hazard classification
The previous near-equal five-class hazard surface is no longer treated as the authoritative hazard product. It has been replaced by fixed numerical FHI thresholds calibrated against historical flood evidence.

### Historical validation added
Independent satellite-derived flood evidence from 2012, 2018 and 2022 is used to evaluate the reconstructed FHI.

### AHP consistency documented
The retained AHP weights have a documented consistency ratio of 0.0093.

### Sensitivity analysis added
The model was tested against 32 AHP weight perturbations and DEM vertical-offset stress scenarios.

### Hazard and risk separated
Physical flood susceptibility is explicitly distinguished from population-informed final risk.

### Causal / hydraulic boundary
The final products do not claim flood depth, annual probability, return period or hydraulic simulation capability.

## Final authoritative thresholds

- Very Low: FHI < 3.20
- Low: 3.20–<3.29
- Moderate: 3.29–<3.37
- High: 3.37–<3.83
- Very High: ≥3.83
